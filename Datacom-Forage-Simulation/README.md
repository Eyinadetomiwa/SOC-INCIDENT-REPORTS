
This folder contains the deliverables completed for the Datacom Cyber Security Operations job simulation on Forage.

Deliverables

### 1. [Cybersecurity Risk Assessment — RetailNova Pty Ltd](Risk%20Assessment%20Report.pdf)

Asset Scope: Mapped Customer Data & E-commerce/Mobile Platform, Core business and Store Systems, and Third Party Integration, including associated threats, vulnerabilities, and mitigation notes.

Risk Assessments (RSK-001 to RSK-003): Evaluated third-party data exposure and customer phishing impersonation (RSK-001), BYOD/phishing/remote access risks (RSK-002), and privilege escalation leading to ransomware disruption of core operations (RSK-003).

Risk Scoring, Treatment & Escalation: Documented inherent and residual likelihood, consequence, and risk ratings alongside existing controls, mitigations, risk owners, and escalation routing (To, Cc, Bcc, and Subject).

## 2. [Security Breach Impact Report — Orion Health Services (`Datacom Security Breach Impact Report.docx`)](Datacom%20Security%20Breach%20Impact%20Report.docx)

Incident Overview (INC-2026-ORION-01): Analyzed a ransomware incident (dated 21st November 2026) beginning with a phishing email containing a malicious Excel attachment, followed by Mimikatz credential harvesting, suspicious login activity from an overseas IP address, and .orionlock file encryption impacting the file server, HR/finance systems, and a partially encrypted backup server.

Impact Assessment Breakdown: Evaluated severity and estimated findings across Data confidentiality, Data integrity, System availability, Regulatory and legal (AU Notifiable Data Breaches scheme and NZ Privacy Act 2020), Reputational/Customer, and Financial and commercial impact dimensions.

Mitigation, Recovery & IOCs: Outlined host isolation, blocking suspicious overseas IPs, enterprise credential resets, backup server auditing, and legal/compliance escalation, along with network, tooling/technique, and host Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) in Appendix A.

Note: Created as part of a simulated cybersecurity job experience exercise on Forage for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes.
